## Featured work

- **[MuleRadar](https://github.com/AbdussamadAI/fintech-hackathon/tree/main/01-mule-radar)**: Detects money-mule rings in live bank transfers using custom CUDA kernels on a transaction graph. **66.7% F1** on the IBM AML benchmark (best published: 64.8%), **99.6% fewer** innocent accounts flagged than a threshold rule, **1.28M transfers/s** on one A100.
- **[ComplaintPulse](https://github.com/AbdussamadAI/fintech-hackathon/tree/main/02-complaint-pulse)**: Multilingual complaint intelligence for banks in English, Pidgin, Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo. Detects emerging incidents **~14 h earlier** than a daily-count rule with **0 false alarms**, and writes analyst briefs with N-ATLaS.
- **[Scalable Multi-GPU Stock Forecasting](https://github.com/AbdussamadAI/MSc-Thesis)** (MSc thesis, *IEEE Access*, under review): Trains BiLSTM and Temporal Fusion Transformer models across many stocks at once. **Up to 36×** faster than CPU, and a multi-stream scheduler that finishes a 32-symbol TFT workload **1.6× faster** on two GPUs.
- **[Kudi Ecosystem](https://github.com/AbdussamadAI/kudi-ecosystem)**: An AI tax assistant using RAG over the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. Built with FastAPI, LlamaIndex, pgvector and Next.js.
- **[Oil-Well Anomaly Detection](https://github.com/AbdussamadAI/oil-well-anomaly-detection)**: Anomaly detection on multivariate sensor time series with TFT and classical ML baselines.
- **[CrewAI Multi-Agent Projects](https://github.com/AbdussamadAI/CrewAI-Multi-agentic-projects)**: Multi-agent LLM workflows where specialised agents work together on a task.
